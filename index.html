Data-quality gate

The model is not always allowed to answer.

A 0–100 trust score decides whether the forecast becomes advice, a request for a manual check, or a pause and an alert.

GreenAdvice allowed — irrigate or prepare, with a reason.
AmberPredict, but ask for a sensor check. No automatic irrigation.
RedPause and alert. Sensor data is not trusted.

3-hour forecast

Soil moisture, 36 steps ahead. A GradientBoosting model forecasts soil moisture; a balanced classifier turns it into Low / Medium / High need.

Energy-aware advice

Water only when power allows. Battery state and generation can turn “irrigate now” into “delay or use manual supply”.
